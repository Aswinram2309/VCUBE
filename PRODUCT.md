## Product Principles

1. **Preserve truth over polish**: Label concept visualizations honestly and never invent client stories, dates, or capabilities to fill space.
2. **Lead with spatial proof**: Let real supplied photography and visualizations demonstrate VCUBE's material sensibility and breadth across residential spaces.
3. **Unify the three disciplines**: Frame Construction, Interior Design, and Vastu Consultation as one coordinated practice rather than disconnected trades.
4. **Lower friction to the first meeting**: Make studio coordinates, phone, WhatsApp, and service-specific enquiries effortless from every major route.
